Planet surface maps

Sun, Mercury, Venus (cloud tops), Earth (day, night lights, clouds, ocean specular), Moon, Mars,
Jupiter, Saturn (+ ring profile), Uranus, Neptune:
  Solar System Scope (https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), based on NASA data.
  Licence: CC BY 4.0. Retrieved from Wikimedia Commons ("Solarsystemscope texture 2k ...").

Ceres:   NASA/JPL-Caltech/UCLA/MPS/DLR/IDA, Dawn framing camera (PIA19625 map, labels and grid removed). Public domain.
Europa:  USGS / NASA, Voyager + Galileo SSI global mosaic. Public domain.
Titan:   NASA/JPL/Space Science Institute, Cassini ISS global map (April 2011). Public domain.
Pluto:   NASA / Johns Hopkins APL / SwRI, New Horizons colour global mosaic. Public domain.

Resized to 2048x1024 (close-up) and 512x256 (map) JPEGs.

Earth close-up in PILOT (earth_4k.jpg on ULTRA, earth_2k.jpg on HIGH and LOW):
  NASA Earth Observatory, Blue Marble: Next Generation (Reto Stöckli, NASA GSFC), November 2004,
  world.200411.3x5400x2700.jpg from https://visibleearth.nasa.gov/images/74192. Public domain.
  Resized to 4096x2048 and 2048x1024 JPEGs.

Live clouds (downloaded at runtime, not bundled):
  NASA Global Imagery Browse Services (GIBS), part of NASA's Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS).
  VIIRS Corrected Reflectance (True Color), Suomi-NPP and NOAA-20. Open data, no restrictions on use.

Submarine cables (public/data/cables.json): TeleGeography Submarine Cable Map, https://www.submarinecablemap.com, CC BY-NC-SA 3.0.
