# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming, be respectful of differing views and experience, give and accept constructive
feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the community. Harassment, insults, sexualised
language or imagery, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable, in the repository or
in any community space tied to the game.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's report-content feature](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
or by opening a private security advisory on this repository. Every report is reviewed and handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues, and may temporarily or permanently ban
contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
